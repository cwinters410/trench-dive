How to play

One answer per prompt. The rarer it is, the deeper you dive.

Every prompt has a list of accepted answers, ranked by how often people say them. Obvious answers barely get you wet. Each list also hides one answer worth the most: the One in a Krillion.

  • 25 seconds per prompt. A wrong guess costs 2 seconds, then you can try again.
  • Spelling can be a little off. Plurals, accents and small typos are fine.
  • Endless dive: no rounds, just 45 seconds of air. Right answers refill it, and rarer answers refill more.
